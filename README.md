Fast-forward to an exact in-game day and time.
Press LEFT ALT + T to open the panel (all keys can be changed in Settings > Controls; look for the "Fast Forward Control" entry):

* See today's day, month, year and time.
* Pick the fast-forward speed (the game's own speeds plus a few extended ones).
* Pick the target day and time, or use the quick presets.
* Optionally let mod "FS25_MarketsAndFutures" price alerts stop the fast-forward when price alert is reached and restore your previous speed.

The previous game speed is restored automatically when the target time is reached.

## Download and install

1. Open the [latest release](https://github.com/jhome16/FS25_FastForwardControl/releases/latest) and download **FS25_FastForwardControl.zip**.
   Direct link: https://github.com/jhome16/FS25_FastForwardControl/releases/latest/download/FS25_FastForwardControl.zip
2. Put the zip file, still zipped, into your mods folder:
   - Windows: `Documents\My Games\FarmingSimulator2025\mods`
   - Mac: `~/Library/Application Support/FarmingSimulator2025/mods`
3. Start the game and enable **Fast Forward Control** in your savegame's mod list.

Do not use the green **Code > Download ZIP** button. That zip has the wrong layout and the game will not load it.
