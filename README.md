Fast-forward to an exact in-game day and time.
Press LEFT ALT + T to open the panel (all keys can be changed in Settings > Controls; look for the "Fast Forward Control" entry):

* See today's day, month, year and time.
* Pick the fast-forward speed (the game's own speeds plus a few extended ones).
* Pick the target day and time, or use the quick presets.
* Optionally let mod "FS25_MarketsAndFutures" price alerts stop the fast-forward when price alert is reached and restore your previous speed.

The previous game speed is restored automatically when the target time is reached.
Detailed mod information can be found at https://github.com/jhome16/FS25_FastForwardControl
